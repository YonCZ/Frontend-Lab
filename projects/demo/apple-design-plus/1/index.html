Attention is a design material
Edited 2 min ago · 412 words · 3 backlinks

Attention is not a budget to spend. It is the material an interface is made of. A screen that asks for everything at once has already spent it.

This is why a quiet surface is not a neutral choice. Removing a control is a decision about where a person can put their mind.

The useful question is not “how do we keep them here?” It is “what are they able to see once they arrive?” That question connects to the spacing effect and to why we reread.

01 Capture

Catch it before it goes.

The thought is usually gone before you find a folder. Noema opens on a shortcut, takes whatever form the idea arrives in, and asks for nothing else.

  • ⌘ ⇧ SpaceFrom anywhere on your Mac. The window closes when the thought lands.
  • PasteDrop a link, a paragraph, or a screenshot. Noema keeps the source with it.
  • SpeakTalk it out on a walk. The transcript lands in your Inbox, ready to cut down.

03 Recall

Ask in your
own words.

You do not need the title, the folder, or the month. Describe what you remember. Noema searches meaning, not spelling, and shows the notes behind every answer.

  • Answers cite the notes they came from.
  • Semantic search runs on your machine.
  • Works offline, including on a plane.

04 Own it

A folder,
not a format.
