    Five places is why it is gone.

    A screenshot in the camera roll. A quote in a chat with yourself. A tab you meant to read. A notebook at your parents' flat. A voice memo you will not scroll back to. Each one is a small, reasonable decision that makes the next search impossible.

    • One search instead of five. Titles, text, sources and kinds sit in the same index.
    • The source stays attached. A quote keeps its page. A link keeps its domain. A call keeps the person.
    • Neighbours do the rest. When the words are gone, walk the note it was kept next to.
