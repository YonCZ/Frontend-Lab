From note to answer

Capture is one keystroke from anywhere. Linking happens as you write. Asking is a sentence, not a search query with the right words in the right order.

1

Write it down

Press ⌘⇧Space on your Mac and type. Cairn saves the note before you finish the thought, then gets out of the way.

Quick capture
The Lisbon studio has better light in the morning

2

It finds the neighbours

Cairn reads the names, dates and subjects in a note and points it at the notes that share them. You keep, edit or ignore each link.

3

Ask for it back

Type the question you actually have. Cairn answers from your notes and shows the three it used, so you can check the answer.

Ask
Why did we keep the deposit open?
So the landlord could repaint the front room before you moved in.
From 2 notes · Deposit terms, 14 Mar

Notes find their neighbours

Every note sits in a map of the notes it touches. Open one and its neighbours are already beside it: the meeting, the person, the paragraph you wrote two years earlier.

  • Backlinks, written plainlyEach mention appears at the foot of the note with the sentence around it.
  • Unlinked mentionsCairn finds notes that talk about the same thing before you have linked them.
  • Read without leavingRest on a note in the map to see its title and date in the bar above.

Files you can open without us

A Cairn library is a folder of Markdown files. Leave it in Dropbox, put it in git, open it in any editor. Cairn adds the links and the answers on top.

There is no import step and no lock-in. If you stop using Cairn tomorrow, every note is still a readable file with its title and date at the top.

  • Plain MarkdownNo database, no proprietary format
  • Your folderiCloud, Dropbox, git, or a disk
  • Sync is optionalUse Cairn on one Mac without an account
