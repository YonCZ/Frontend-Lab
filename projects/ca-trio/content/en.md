## Booking

Orders go through a form on each trip page. Fields: name, email, phone, number of participants and their names, and an optional note. Customers use the note to request travel insurance and to supply participant names.

The form has a spam trap field and Google reCAPTCHA. The rebuild needs a real submission path or a documented mock, not a dead form.

## Cancellation terms

The customer pays a cancellation fee on the ordered services:

- 15 percent when cancelled more than 30 days before departure
- 30 percent when cancelled more than 14 days before departure
- 60 percent when cancelled more than 3 days before departure
- 100 percent when cancelled later than 3 days before departure

No fee applies for a death in the family, serious illness, hospital stay, natural disaster, accident or another unforeseeably serious reason.
